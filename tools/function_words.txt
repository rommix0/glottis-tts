# Function words: flagged in the dictionary so prosody leaves them unaccented and short.
# Closed-class words that are normally unaccented in running speech. Words that usually take an accent are left
# out on purpose: negatives (not, never, can't, don't), quantifiers (all, every, no, none, many), possessive
# pronouns (mine, yours), question words (what, why, how), and particles that end phrasal verbs (up, out, off, down).
